# P12 source freeze

Primary conduct authority: Khodyakov, Grant, Kroger, and Bauman, *RAND Methodological Guidance for Conducting and Critically Appraising Delphi Panels*, RAND TL-A3082-1, 2023, DOI [10.7249/TLA3082-1](https://doi.org/10.7249/TLA3082-1). Frozen locators: ch. 2 characteristics/types; ch. 3 design and panel eligibility; ch. 4 collection and moderation; ch. 5 stability, consensus, comments and missingness; ch. 6 reporting and limitations; appendices A–B. [RAND record and PDF](https://www.rand.org/pubs/tools/TLA3082-1.html), accessed 2026-08-13.

Corroborating source: Linstone and Turoff (eds.), *The Delphi Method: Techniques and Applications*, 2002 digital edition, especially introductory design chapters on anonymity, iteration, controlled feedback, and statistical group response. [NJIT-hosted edition](https://web.njit.edu/~turoff/pubs/delphibook/), accessed 2026-08-13.

Variant guard: exactly three anonymous questionnaire rounds are frozen. Round feedback includes aggregate distributions and anonymized rationales; panelists retain their own judgments. Consensus is a described panel state, not truth, representativeness, or a policy decision; stable dissensus is a valid output.
