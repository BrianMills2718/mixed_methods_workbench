1.Goal: what this plan is for and why

Outcome. Brian can open the public PsychosisBank review and judge each finding against its sources and its publication block, and can read which method steps the crosswalk found shared across methods.

North star

A text-centered mixed-methods workbench that joins method engines' exports through typed contracts, built in thin versioned slices (docs/adr/0002_broad_north_star_versioned_thin_slices.md).

Thesis

Methods stay in their own repositories; the workbench reads their exports, keeps claim limits, and joins them only where a study needs it.

What would prove it wrong

Joining two producers' exports for a real study needs method logic the producers do not export, or the crosswalk's different-capability verdicts are wrong in a way a real study exposes.

Not for

Running a method itself, a universal schema or method planner, or publishing an unreviewed causal conclusion.
