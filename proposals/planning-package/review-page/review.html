1.Goal: what this plan is for and why

Outcome. The Mixed Methods Workbench is finished to 1.0 as a general tool in Brian's broader vision, not for one use case: on two rights-clear interview corpora a person can open a study page, follow every qualitative claim to its passage, see a quantitative strand measured and checked against the authors' own coding, read a joint display with bounded meta-inferences and a gated causal check, review and export everything with full traces, and read on a benchmark page what works, what fails and what it cost. The public PsychosisBank review stays served from committed files.

North star

Brian, 2026-11-06: "on mixed mtheod work bench finsih that is not for a specific use case it is part of my broader vision". Finish the workbench to 1.0 as a general tool: a governed corpus, a qualitative strand read from Qualitative Coding, a quantitative strand checked against human coding, a joint display with bounded meta-inference, a gated causal check, review and export, proven on two rights-clear corpora (docs/plans/008_finish_workbench.md).

Thesis

Methods stay in their own repositories; the workbench freezes the corpus, reads each method's export, owns the narrow quantitative adapter no repository has, and joins the strands with every claim traceable to its passage.

What would prove it wrong

On a second rights-clear corpus added without code changes, the quantitative strand fails to agree with the authors' coding beyond chance, or the planted failures pass the checks unnoticed.

Not for

Running a qualitative method inside the workbench, a universal schema or method planner, contacting corpus authors, or publishing an unreviewed causal conclusion.
