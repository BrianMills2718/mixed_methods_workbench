1.Goal: what this plan is for and why

Outcome. The Mixed Methods Workbench is finished to 1.0 as a general tool and as part of Brian's broader evidence-to-action vision, not for one use case: each study is one investigation record, the investigation spine the repository's evidence-to-action product map describes, running from question and evidence to reviewed findings and next actions; on two rights-clear interview corpora a person can open that record, follow every qualitative claim to its passage, see a quantitative strand measured and checked against the authors' own coding, read a joint display with bounded meta-inferences and a gated causal check, review and export everything with full traces, and read on a benchmark page what works, what fails and what it cost. The public PsychosisBank review stays served from committed files.

North star

Brian, 2026-11-06: "on mixed mtheod work bench finsih that is not for a specific use case it is part of my broader vision". The direction: a general mixed-methods workbench in his evidence-to-action product, where each study is one investigation record and every joined claim can be followed to its passage, while each method stays in its own repository.

Thesis

Integration is the workbench's own value: given only a study definition and the producers' exports (Qualitative Coding's run on each corpus happens in its own repository first, Q21), it can measure a qualitative category so that the measure agrees with the corpus authors' own coding on held-out interviews, join the two strands into bounded meta-inferences a person reviews, and catch planted faults, without any method being rebuilt here.

What would prove it wrong

On a second rights-clear corpus that differs in subject, team and coding tool, added with data only, the measure agrees with the authors' coding no better than the shuffled-label control, the planted faults pass unnoticed, or adding the corpus needs code written for it.

Not for

Running a qualitative method inside the workbench, a universal schema or method planner, contacting corpus authors, or publishing an unreviewed causal conclusion.
