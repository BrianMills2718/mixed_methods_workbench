## Researcher-visible outcome

A researcher can open one NYC Congestion Relief Zone investigation and follow:

```text
frozen question and sources
-> reviewed prediction and hearing evidence
-> method-owned qualitative description
-> reproducible quantitative comparison
-> explicit integration and policy appraisal
-> bounded retain / change / monitor conclusions
```

Every visible finding steps down to exact evidence, transformation, review
state, and method owner. The product refuses causal, prevalence,
representativeness, and recommendation claims that the available evidence does
not support.

## Capability boundaries

The first executable seam deliberately separates three operations:

1. **Structured extraction** — exact source unit plus a Pydantic schema and
   prompt asset produces candidate records and a durable `llm_client` trace.
2. **Evidence binding** — deterministic code verifies source identity, text
   hashes, locator semantics, and selection-in-context. It makes no analytic
   judgment.
3. **Review and promotion** — a candidate remains visibly pending or rejected
   until an attributable human decision promotes the supported fields.

Qualitative Coding still owns qualitative description, comparison, negative
cases, reflexivity, and adequacy limits. A successful extraction is not a QC
analysis. The Workbench owns the policy-prediction and hearing-concern consumer
records, quantitative receipt, joint display, integration logic, and product
claim boundaries. Data Contracts adoption remains deferred until a second
authentic compatible consumer demonstrates a stable cross-project seam.

## Execution sequence and parallelism

`NYC-EXTRACT-1` is the immediate critical-path unit. `NYC-QUANT-1` is a
non-overlapping ready lane and may run in parallel when another contributor is
available. `NYC-QC-1` starts only after the extraction/anchor/review contract is
accepted, because the QC export must bind to that exact evidence semantics
without copying Workbench product logic into the producer.

```text
NYC-EXTRACT-1 -----> NYC-QC-1 -----+
        |                           |
        +---------------------------+--> NYC-INTEGRATE-1 --> NYC-MVP-REVIEW-1
NYC-QUANT-1 ------------------------+

NYC-INTEGRATE-1 --optional evidence--> NYC-CONTRACT-PROMOTION-1 (deferred)
```

Only `NYC-INTEGRATE-1` edits the final cross-strand presentation after the
three inputs converge. Producer lanes do not edit the Workbench UI. This keeps
parallel work independent and gives one owner the semantic integration point.

## MVP acceptance boundary

The MVP is not complete until all of the following are observed at the exact
integrated revision:

- at least one authentic, non-cached, fully traced structured extraction;
- exact byte and anchor verification with both-sign failure controls;
- attributable human disposition of the first prediction, concern, and
  quantitative comparison;
- an authentic bounded QC Describe artifact over all six frozen hearings,
  including variation, contradiction/negative cases, reflexivity, and a
  no-prevalence limit;
- a reproducible predicted-versus-observed quantitative comparison that keeps
  agency publication-time values separate from the current data snapshot and
  refuses causal attribution;
- a joint display that preserves evidence origin, method owner, temporal scope,
  version, review state, and unresolved questions;
- a policy appraisal whose value judgments are explicit and human-owned;
- one fresh-browser walkthrough, matching typed JSON, inspectable export, and
  a recorded human comprehension/methodological review.

The MVP does not require Process Tracing, Theory Forge, the full MTA archive, a
universal method ontology, deployment, a population-effect estimate, or Data
Contracts promotion.

## Stop and reset conditions

- Stop if exact source bytes or frozen query receipts no longer verify.
- Reject rather than repair silently when model text cannot bind uniquely to
  its declared source unit.
- Keep candidates pending when human review has not occurred.
- Reset the quantitative claim if a new data version is substituted without a
  versioned comparison receipt.
- Narrow or stop the QC run if the six-hearing corpus cannot be processed with
  its denominator and negative-case search intact.
- Do not start another method-catalog or shared-schema lane to avoid an observed
  vertical failure.
