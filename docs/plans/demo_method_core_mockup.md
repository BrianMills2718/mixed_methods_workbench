## Shared Question and Sources

Question: **Why did the fictional Harbor Agency adopt the contested relocation
policy?**

Source packet: three invented documents with stable hashes and exact spans:
one leadership memo, one staff interview, and one dated implementation log.
The packet deliberately contains both resource-pressure language and a
contrary passage describing a pre-existing safety rationale.

## At-a-Glance Review

| Lane | What this method can say in the demo | What it cannot say |
|---|---|---|
| QC | How participants/documents frame relocation; recurring patterns, exceptions, and reviewed interpretations. | Which causal explanation is comparatively strongest. |
| PT | How observations discriminate among resource-pressure, safety, and residual explanations within this case. | Population effects or universal causal truth. |
| GT-inspired | How categories and relationships develop through comparison; what remains underdeveloped and what source to seek next. | That a substantive theory is saturated or methodologically validated. |

## QC Lane

adaptation, while the leadership memo frames it as safety continuity.**

- Pattern: two competing frames, not one consensus theme.
- Supporting anchors: interview §18–22; implementation log lines 40–47.
- Contrary/qualifying anchor: leadership memo characters 312–489.
- Review: `needs_human_review`.
- Step down: finding → code applications → exact passages → source hashes.

## Grounded-Theory-Inspired Lane

- Properties: source of threat; internal translator; urgency; action type.

- Prohibited reading: “The category is saturated” or “the substantive theory

## Review Decision

The reviewer can:

- retain, revise, or reject each method-native finding;
- inspect its native object and exact source;
- accept/reject individual cross-method links;
- record disagreement or silence without forcing resolution;
- request the next discriminating source.

The packet cannot produce one overall confidence score or a publishable
synthesis. Its successful readout is that the three methods are simultaneously
comparable and unmistakably different.

## Failure States to Preview

- QC anchor missing → finding blocked, not rendered as supported.
- PT residual hypothesis absent → entire PT packet rejected.
- GT diagnostic labeled “saturated” → contract rejected as an overclaim.
- Unsupported producer major version → compatible consumer fails loudly.
- Cross-method link uses `supports` or numeric weight → link rejected.
- Any source or finding labeled real → demo claim-limit gate fails.
