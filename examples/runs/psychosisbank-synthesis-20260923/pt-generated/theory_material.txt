# Reviewed QC proposition supplied before automated case extraction

Proposition: P5 / p5-v1
Reviewed wording: Where cleaning, anonymization, repository curation, permissions, priority concerns, criticism, and publication pressure remain individualized, researchers may favor delayed, restricted, or minimum-compliance disclosure even while valuing openness.
Research question: What best explains the PsychosisBank and TalkBank team's choice of graduated and controlled access for psychiatric speech data: burden and risk deliberation, direct external rules, or stable prior preference and routine?
Observation unit: One research project's disclosure decision over time.

Condition: Cleaning, anonymization, repository curation, permissions, priority concerns, criticism, and publication pressure remain individualized.
Reviewed analytic process: Researchers calculate labour, risk, timing, and exposure in disclosure decisions.
Mechanism: Researchers weigh labour, risk, timing, and exposure in a disclosure decision.
Possible strategies: delayed disclosure, restricted disclosure, minimum compliance
Boundary: Researchers may still value openness while choosing a restrictive disclosure strategy.
Additional reviewed boundaries:
- Support, mandates, and established workflows can reduce the individualized burden.
- The form of risk differs for sensitive, copyrighted, and long-labour datasets.

Observable implications inherited from the reviewed QC design:
- Burden or risk is recognized before the restrictive strategy is selected.
- Decision records show weighing of openness against permissions, labour, priority, scrutiny, or publication pressure.
- Removing or absorbing the salient burden changes the considered or selected strategy within a comparable case.

Rival explanations inherited from the reviewed QC design:
- The strategy reflects stable researcher preference rather than project-specific deliberation.
- Journal, funder, ethics, legal, or repository rules determine the strategy directly.
- The apparent burden is a retrospective justification for a decision made for another reason.

Selection disclosure: The proposition and rival families predate this case packet; the case was selected after an analyst scan of public source availability.
Treat P5 as a candidate process explanation, not as evidence. Preserve the burden/risk -> deliberative weighing -> disclosure-strategy sequence, compare it with whole-story rivals, and do not treat source availability or case selection as confirmation.
