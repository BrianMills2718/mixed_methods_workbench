# Phase 1 frozen methodology sources

Status: frozen before decomposition

Accessed: 2026-08-13

Each pilot uses one primary source and one corroborating source. The second
source may clarify reporting or inference, but it does not silently create a
composite method. Where the sources differ, the primary source controls the
variant and the difference is recorded.

## Systematic review

Primary: Higgins JPT, Thomas J, Chandler J, Cumpston M, Li T, Page MJ, Welch VA
(eds.). *Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions*, version
6.5.1, 2025, especially chapters 1–10.
<https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current>

Why selected: the current official Cochrane guide specifies the conduct of an
intervention-effect review from question and eligibility through search,
selection, data collection, risk of bias, synthesis, and meta-analysis.

Corroborating: Page MJ et al. PRISMA 2020 statement and expanded checklist.
<https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020>

Boundary: PRISMA is a reporting guideline, not a substitute for the Cochrane
conduct method. It supplies reporting and flow-accounting obligations only.

## Constructivist grounded theory

Primary: Charmaz K. *Constructing Grounded Theory*, 3rd ed. SAGE, 2025,
chapters 2–9.
<https://uk.sagepub.com/en-gb/asi/constructing-grounded-theory/book255601>

Why selected: the current edition specifies rich-data collection, initial and
focused coding, memo-writing, theoretical sampling, saturation/adequacy,
sorting, integration, and theorizing within the constructivist variant.

Corroborating: Charmaz K. “With Constructivist Grounded Theory You Can’t Hide.”
*Qualitative Inquiry* 26(2), 2020.
<https://doi.org/10.1177/1077800419879081>

Boundary: this pilot does not import Straussian axial-coding requirements or
claim that a fixed corpus can achieve theoretical sampling merely by rereading.

## Theory-testing process tracing

Primary: Beach D, Pedersen RB. *Process-Tracing Methods: Foundations and
Guidelines*, 2nd ed. University of Michigan Press, 2019, theory-testing variant.
<https://doi.org/10.3998/mpub.10072208>

Why selected: it distinguishes process-tracing variants and specifies
theory-testing work around causal mechanisms, case evidence, and inferential
tests.

Corroborating: Fairfield T, Charman AE. *Social Inquiry and Bayesian Inference:
Rethinking Qualitative Research*. Cambridge University Press, 2022.
<https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108377522>

Boundary: Bayesian likelihood reasoning clarifies comparative evidential weight;
it does not turn within-case evidence into a population treatment-effect
estimate or make subjective likelihood judgments method-neutral.

## Randomized causal-effect estimation

Primary: International Council for Harmonisation. ICH E9, *Statistical
Principles for Clinical Trials*, 1998, with E9(R1), *Estimands and Sensitivity
Analysis in Clinical Trials*, 2019/2021 final guidance.
<https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/ich-e9-statistical-principles-clinical-trials-scientific-guideline>

Why selected: E9/E9(R1) links trial objective, estimand, design, conduct,
analysis, sensitivity analysis, and interpretation.

Corroborating: Hopewell S et al. “CONSORT 2025 statement: updated guideline for
reporting randomised trials.” *BMJ* 389:e081123, 2025.
<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2024-081123>

Boundary: CONSORT 2025 controls transparent reporting and participant flow; it
does not replace the prespecified estimand or statistical-analysis plan.

## Policy option appraisal

Primary: HM Treasury. *The Green Book 2026: Appraisal in Central Government*,
updated 5 February 2026.
<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book-appraisal-and-evaluation-in-central-government/the-green-book-2026>

Why selected: it specifies rationale/objectives, longlisting, shortlisting,
social cost-benefit or cost-effectiveness analysis, uncertainty,
distributional analysis, presentation, preferred-option advice, and the
ROAMEF feedback cycle.

Corroborating: Department for Communities and Local Government. *Multi-criteria
analysis: a manual*, amended 2009.
<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/multi-criteria-analysis-manual-for-making-government-policy>

Boundary: MCDA supplies a structured treatment of criteria, scoring, weighting,
and sensitivity where monetization is incomplete. It does not authorize hiding
value judgments inside an apparently objective aggregate score.

## Hostile counterexample: agent-based policy simulation

Primary: Grimm V et al. “The ODD Protocol for Describing Agent-Based and Other
Simulation Models: A Second Update to Improve Clarity, Replication, and
Structural Realism.” *JASSS* 23(2), 2020.
<https://doi.org/10.18564/jasss.4259>

Why selected: ODD specifies purpose/patterns, entities and state variables,
process scheduling, design concepts, initialization, input data, and submodels,
while separating model description from implementation.

Corroborating: Azzini I, Listorti G, Mara T, Rosati R. *Uncertainty and
Sensitivity Analysis for Policy Decision Making*. European Commission Joint
Research Centre, 2020. DOI 10.2760/922129.
<https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC122132>

Boundary: simulation output is model-conditional generated evidence. It is not
an observation of the policy world and cannot validate its own structural
assumptions.
