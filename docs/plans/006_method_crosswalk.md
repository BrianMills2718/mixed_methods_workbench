## Outputs

| Path | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `phase4/crosswalk.yaml` | One row per Level 2 move and Level 3 action (171): its method-standard anchors (copied from the record), whether it is a statistical step, and for statistical steps the STATO term(s) for what it computes, with a one-line reason; a non-statistical step that uses a statistical-looking verb says why it is not one |
| `phase4/collisions.json` | Generated by `scripts/crosswalk_phase4.py` from the records and the crosswalk: §9 groups by canonical signature, the secondary verb-only and type-pair groupings, and groups of steps sharing an exact STATO term |
| `phase5/adjudication.yaml` | One §10 verdict for every group with members from two or more methods, each with its written justification |

## Rules

1. **STATO terms must exist** in the pinned release and must not be deprecated. Unknown ids fail the check.
2. **Only an exact shared term matches automatically.** A shared ancestor is reported but does not match: for
   example the RCT mean difference (`STATO:0000457`) and difference in differences (`STATO:0000665`) are siblings
   under "absolute difference" (`STATO:0000614`), so they are a §10 question, not an automatic match.
3. **§10 verdicts** are `same_capability`, `shared_shell` or `different_capability`; the default when uncertain is
   `different_capability`. A large divergence in permitted conclusions disqualifies `same_capability`; members in
   different topological roles (a single pass versus a required cycle with human termination) are downgraded; every
   `same_capability` names a method outside the group and argues whether it could use the capability; every
   `shared_shell` says what the shared code would do and what it delegates. A verdict without a justification fails.
4. **Phase 3 records are read-only.** A defect found in one is returned to its lane through a new Phase 3 review, not
   patched here.

## Checks

`scripts/crosswalk_phase4.py --check` regenerates `collisions.json` and checks the crosswalk (every step present
once, anchors equal the record's, STATO ids valid) and the adjudication (one verdict per multi-method group, valid
verdict, justification present, the rule-3 requirements). `tests/test_crosswalk_phase4.py` covers it.

## Non-claims

No coverage or effort assessment (spec §11, Phase 6), no capability promotion, no shared schema adoption, no
implementation. A `same_capability` verdict is a candidate for later design, not a decision to build.
