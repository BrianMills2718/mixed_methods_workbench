## Primary conduct authority

Higgins JPT, Thomas J, Chandler J, Cumpston M, Li T, Page MJ, Welch VA,
editors. *Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions*, version
6.5, Cochrane, 2024. [Official current handbook](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current).

Pinned sections: chapter 2 (scope and question), chapter 3 (prespecified
eligibility and grouping), chapter 4 (searching, report linkage, and study
selection), chapter 5 (data collection), chapter 8 (RoB 2), chapter 9
(preparing synthesis), chapter 10 (meta-analysis), chapter 13 (assessing
risk of bias due to missing results in a synthesis), chapter 14 (completing
'Summary of findings' tables and grading the certainty of the evidence),
chapter 15 (interpreting results and drawing conclusions), and chapter III
(reporting the review). The official index identifies version 6.5 and these
chapter boundaries; chapters 14 and 15 were confirmed against the current
contents page (https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current)
on 2026-10-08 and belong to the same 6.5 edition already pinned. Chapter 3 requires prespecification of PICO,
study eligibility, and synthesis grouping; chapter 4 requires a thorough,
objective, reproducible multi-source search and treats studies rather than
reports as the unit of interest.

## Corroborating reporting authority

Page MJ et al. “The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting
systematic reviews.” *BMJ* 2021;372:n71. [PRISMA statement](https://www.bmj.com/content/372/bmj.n71).
Pinned items: 5–15 (methods, including item 14 reporting bias assessment and
item 15 certainty assessment), 16–23 (results, including item 20 results of
syntheses, item 21 reporting biases and item 22 certainty of evidence, and
item 23 discussion), and 24–27 (registration, support, interests, and
data/material availability). PRISMA corroborates
durable protocol, selection, exclusion, and synthesis reporting; it is not the
sole conduct authority.

## Source-limit note

This freeze covers randomized-intervention reviews and conditional pairwise
meta-analysis only. It does not license network, qualitative, realist, or
observational synthesis variants.
