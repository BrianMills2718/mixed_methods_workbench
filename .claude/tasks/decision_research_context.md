# Decision Research Context: GOV Case and First Quantitative-Text Strand

Date: 2026-07-12
Status: frozen shared context for two documentation-only research lanes

## Mission

Narrow two genuine architecture decisions that block the long-term
mixed-methods program:

1. which public case and permitted source use should anchor the first governed
   multi-method qualitative slice; and
2. which bounded quantitative-text task and owner boundary should anchor the
   first genuine qualitative-quantitative slice.

The parent will turn the evidence into decision briefs for Brian. Research does
not authorize either implementation.

## Current Truth

- `mixed_methods_workbench` is the integration authority; producer repositories
  retain their method invariants and strict exports.
- Current mode is documentation-only. No implementation slice or product
  version is active.
- `T0-PROV` is independently signed off only for synthetic inventory
  provenance. Fixture shapes remain C, broader T0 remains F, and no producer,
  method, mixed-methods, or SOTA claim moved.
- Governance precedes real QC/PT exports: `T0 -> GOV -> QC + PT -> R01`.
- QC plus PT is multi-method qualitative, not mixed methods.
- The first true mixed-methods claim requires a validated quantitative strand
  and explicit connect/build/merge/embed/transform operation, joint display or
  equivalent, divergence disposition, and bounded meta-inference.
- No quantitative-text task, instrument, adapter owner, or evaluation design is
  selected. The roadmap recommends established libraries behind a narrow,
  project-specific adapter before considering shared infrastructure.
- The proposed first public case is an 18 Brumaire source packet. It is only a
  planning recommendation and must be compared against at least one plausible
  alternative. No source license, corpus denominator, translation/edition,
  anchor policy, or publication scope is currently approved.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

- Research only. Do not edit canonical docs, code, fixtures, schemas, producer
  repositories, or this context file.
- Write only the assigned report under `.claude/tasks/` in this worktree.
- Do not interpret a recommendation as human approval or named implementation
  authorization.
- Do not invent a universal quality or SOTA score. Claims must be task-,
  construct-, method-, corpus-, language-, and use-bounded.
- Prefer primary research and official/authoritative project, standard,
  repository, legal-rights, or archive sources. Record direct URLs and access
  date 2026-07-12.
- Separate observed fact, inference, and recommendation. Record nulls,
  conflicts, uncertainty, and freshness triggers.
- Do not treat a site's public accessibility as permission to redistribute,
  modify, process, or publish a derivative corpus. Distinguish underlying work,
  edition/translation, site terms, and intended artifact use.
- Do not treat topic coherence, face-valid labels, or accuracy alone as
  construct validity. Quantitative text requires a declared construct, unit,
  population/corpus, intended inference/use, error costs, baseline, held-out
  evidence, uncertainty, item-level traceability, and qualitative error review.

## Lane A: First Case and Source Governance

Audit the 18 Brumaire proposal and compare at least one viable public
alternative. For every candidate, evaluate:

- research question and why QC plus PT can both contribute without collapsing
  their method boundaries;
- exact corpus denominator, edition/translation/version, authoritative source,
  stable retrieval, and content hashing;
- underlying-work status, edition/translation rights, host terms, permitted
  local processing, excerpt/full-text redistribution, and publication limits;
- sensitivity, privacy, community/ethical obligations, and any access tier;
- stable quote/position anchors, known OCR/transcription drift, and whether
  deterministic anchor recovery is feasible;
- rival hypotheses, conflicting evidence, absences, and enough source diversity
  for a diagnostic within-case test;
- contamination risk, practical size, reviewer burden, and replaceability;
- exact unresolved human/legal judgments and a minimal GOV acceptance packet.

Return a comparison matrix, recommended default (or explicit no-safe-default),
confidence, disqualifiers, and the narrow decision Brian must make. This is not
legal advice; distinguish a documented rights basis from a legal conclusion.

## Lane B: First Quantitative-Text Strand

Compare at least three bounded first-task candidates aligned to an exploratory
sequential design, including:

- a transparent dictionary/count or prevalence measurement;
- a supervised annotation/classification or ordinal measurement; and
- an unsupervised topic/embedding-based option as a negative or conditional
  comparator rather than an assumed default.

For each candidate define the construct, unit, corpus/population, intended use
and prohibited use, qualitative-to-quantitative build operation, ground-truth
or validation strategy, split/leakage controls, simple and strongest relevant
incumbents, metrics and uncertainty, item-level linkage, error slices, reviewer
burden, and failure cost. Compare owner boundaries:

- a narrow adapter owned by `mixed_methods_workbench` around established
  libraries;
- an adapter owned by an existing producer; and
- a new shared engine only after repeated real slices.

Return a comparison matrix, recommended first task and owner boundary (or
explicit no-safe-default), decision criteria, disqualifiers, and the narrow
decision Brian must make. Do not select a specific construct that the governed
case cannot support; express any case-dependent recommendation conditionally.
